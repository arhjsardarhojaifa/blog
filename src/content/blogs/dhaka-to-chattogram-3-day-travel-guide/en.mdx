## Introduction: Exploring Chattogram in Three Days

Chattogram is one of those destinations in Bangladesh where you can experience different landscapes and lifestyles within a single trip. From hills and forest trails to waterfalls, beaches, busy city streets and a nearby island, the region offers a variety of experiences for travellers.

I travelled solo from Dhaka to the Chattogram region with a simple goal: explore as many places as possible while keeping my expenses under control. I chose trains for most of my intercity travel because I considered them a relatively affordable and convenient option.

Over three days, my journey included Melkhum Trail in Mirsarai, Chandranath Hill and Sitakunda Eco Park, Bashbaria Sea Beach, several destinations in Chattogram city, and Sandwip Island.

Food was another part of the experience. I tried Chattogram's famous mezban meal and Sandwip's buffalo-milk yogurt. I would also recommend trying fresh coconut water while visiting Sandwip.

However, the journey was not entirely about beautiful destinations and local food. At several places, I encountered sellers and service providers who charged me more because I appeared to be a tourist. Some interactions also became uncomfortable when people repeatedly pressured me to buy their products or services.

In this guide, I will share my three-day itinerary, the expenses I can account for, accommodation costs, food experiences and practical safety tips for anyone planning a similar trip.

## Chattogram Trip at a Glance

* *Duration:* 3 days
* *Travel style:* Solo budget travel
* *Transport:* Train, local transport and speedboat
* *Main destinations:* Melkhum Trail, Chandranath Hill, Sitakunda Eco Park, Bashbaria Sea Beach, Chattogram city and Sandwip
* *Accommodation:* One night near Sitakunda Bazar and one night in Chattogram city
* *Food highlights:* Mezban, buffalo-milk yogurt and fresh coconut water
* *Known expenses:* BDT 5,850, excluding yogurt, other meals, local transport and additional unrecorded expenses

The amount above is not my complete trip budget. It is the sum of the expenses for which I have specific figures.

## Day 1: Melkhum Trail, Chandranath Hill, Sitakunda Eco Park and Bashbaria Beach

### Travelling from Dhaka

I chose the train for my journey towards Chattogram. My ticket cost approximately BDT 400 because I got off at Feni Railway Station instead of Chattogram Railway Station. Based on my fare estimate, travelling all the way to Chattogram would have cost around BDT 450.

I chose the train mainly to keep my travel expenses relatively low. Travellers who prefer a more comfortable journey may also consider buses. Based on the information available to me, bus fares may range from approximately BDT 700 to BDT 1,400, depending on the service and travel conditions.

These are my reported fare figures, not verified current prices. It is best to check the latest fares before travelling.

### Exploring Melkhum Trail in Mirsarai

My first major destination was Melkhum Trail in Mirsarai. After completing the trail, I continued towards Sitakunda to visit Chandranath Hill, Sitakunda Eco Park and the waterfall inside the park.

When planning a trail or hill excursion, knowing the destination alone is not enough. You should also consider transport arrangements, walking time, weather conditions, suitable footwear and your return journey.

Travelling solo makes preparation even more important because you need to manage your own time, belongings and safety.

I have not included an exact trail duration or invented details about the route because I have not recorded those specifics here.

### Chandranath Hill and Sitakunda Eco Park

After Melkhum Trail, I visited Chandranath Hill and Sitakunda Eco Park. I also explored the waterfall inside the park.

Combining a trail, a hill, a park and a waterfall in one day requires careful time management. Travel between destinations is only one part of the schedule; walking, resting and returning before it gets dark also need consideration.

If you plan to follow a similar route, check local transport options and the condition of the trails beforehand. Allow enough time to return safely rather than trying to fit in every destination at any cost.

### Bashbaria Sea Beach

Another destination on my first day was Bashbaria Sea Beach. Including the beach after visiting the hills, trail and waterfall added another type of landscape to my itinerary.

However, visiting several destinations in one day can be demanding. Before copying this itinerary, consider your physical fitness, available daylight, transport schedules and the time needed at each location.

I spent the first night at a hotel near Sitakunda Bazar. My accommodation cost was BDT 1,000 for one night.

## Day 2: Chattogram City, Bayezid Bostami Shrine, Patenga Beach and Karnaphuli Tunnel

After spending the first day exploring natural destinations, I dedicated my second day to Chattogram city and nearby attractions.

My itinerary included:

* Bayezid Bostami Shrine
* Patenga Sea Beach
* Karnaphuli Tunnel
* Chattogram Railway Museum
* Various local areas of the city

I have not recorded the exact order or time spent at each destination, so this list represents the places I visited rather than a verified hour-by-hour schedule.

### Exploring the City

One of the interesting aspects of visiting Chattogram is the contrast between its natural landscapes and urban life. Beyond hills, trails and beaches, exploring local neighbourhoods gives you another perspective on the region.

When travelling around the city, consider traffic, transport costs and the locations of your planned stops. Grouping nearby destinations together can help reduce unnecessary travel and make better use of your time.

I spent my second night at a hotel in Chattogram city. The accommodation cost was BDT 1,500 for one night.

## Day 3: A Trip to Sandwip Island

My third day was dedicated to Sandwip Island. I travelled by speedboat from Kumira Ghat. My reported fare was BDT 300 for the onward journey and another BDT 300 for the return trip.

After reaching Sandwip, I rented a motorbike to explore different parts of the island. Renting it for eight hours cost me BDT 1,400.

### Exploring Sandwip by Motorbike

Renting a motorbike allowed me to explore the island according to my own schedule. It was also one of the more significant individual expenses of this part of the trip.

If you plan to explore Sandwip in a similar way, include the cost of transport on the island in addition to your speedboat fares.

Before renting a motorbike, clarify the rental period, total price, fuel arrangements and return conditions. My eight-hour rental cost BDT 1,400, but prices may vary depending on the provider and conditions.

### Sandwip's Buffalo-Milk Yogurt and Fresh Coconut Water

During my visit, I tried Sandwip's buffalo-milk yogurt. The price I encountered was approximately BDT 200 per kilogram.

I would also recommend trying fresh coconut water when visiting Sandwip. In my experience, fresh coconuts were available at relatively affordable prices compared with many other places I had visited. Actual prices can vary by location, season and seller.

After exploring the island, I returned to Chattogram and took a train from Chattogram Railway Station to Dhaka. My return train fare was approximately BDT 450.

Mezban is one of the best-known culinary traditions associated with Chattogram. During my trip, I tried a mezban meal at a relatively good restaurant, where I spent approximately BDT 500.

This amount reflects my own experience. Prices at other restaurants may differ depending on the portion size, establishment and meal options.

### Buffalo-Milk Yogurt in Sandwip

Sandwip's buffalo-milk yogurt was another local food I tried. Based on my experience, the price was approximately BDT 200 per kilogram.

If you plan to buy some, check the current price and quantity with the seller before making your purchase.

### Fresh Coconut Water

Fresh coconut water is another thing I would recommend trying in Sandwip. I found it to be a worthwhile part of the local food experience.

I do not have an exact price to report, so I have not included a specific amount in the budget.

### Eating at Local Restaurants

Apart from mezban and yogurt, I ate at ordinary local restaurants for most of my meals. I do not have a complete record of those expenses.

One practical recommendation concerning drinking water: if you are unsure about the safety of the local water supply, choose sealed drinking water from a reliable source. Check that the seal is intact before drinking it. This may be particularly important for travellers who are prone to stomach problems when drinking unfamiliar water.

## Uncomfortable Experiences and Tourist Scams

One of the less pleasant aspects of my trip was how some sellers and service providers treated me after recognising me as a tourist.

In my experience, some people quoted higher prices for products or services and repeatedly pressured me to buy from them. On a few occasions, the behaviour became particularly uncomfortable, including unwanted physical pulling or people following me while trying to persuade me to purchase something.

As a result, I sometimes paid more than I considered a fair price.

Such encounters can be stressful, especially when travelling alone. However, these are my personal experiences and should not be taken to mean that everyone in Chattogram behaves this way. Travellers can encounter both helpful and unhelpful people in any destination.

### How to Handle Uncomfortable Situations

1. *Ask the price before agreeing to anything.* Compare prices between different sellers or service providers when possible.

2. *Do not feel obligated to purchase something.* If someone pressures you, politely decline and move away if it is safe to do so.

3. *Confirm transport fares in advance.* Agree on the destination and fare before starting a journey whenever possible.

4. *Keep your money and valuables secure.* Avoid displaying large amounts of cash unnecessarily.

5. *Move towards a public place if you feel unsafe.* Seek assistance from a trusted person or the appropriate local authorities if necessary.

## Solo Travel Safety Tips for Chattogram

Travelling alone requires extra attention to personal belongings, transport and unfamiliar surroundings. I did not personally experience every type of problem listed below; some concerns were based on accounts I heard from other travellers.

* *Keep your luggage close on trains.* Do not casually hand over valuables to strangers.
* *Never extend your phone or other belongings through a moving train window.* This can lead to loss or injury.
* *Be alert in crowded city areas.* Pay attention to your pockets and bags, especially around railway stations and busy public places.
* *Learn about local conditions before exploring unfamiliar rural areas alone.* Consider the route, return transport and mobile connectivity.
* *Check the weather and trail conditions before visiting hills, trails and waterfalls.* Avoid taking risks beyond your abilities.
* *Confirm the speedboat schedule and return arrangements before visiting Sandwip.* Boat services may change because of weather or local conditions.
* *Keep some emergency money separate from your regular travel budget.* Unexpected transport or accommodation costs can arise.

## How to Plan a Three-Day Chattogram Trip

My itinerary combined hills, a trail, a waterfall, city attractions, beaches and an island. However, trying to visit too many destinations in a short period can be tiring and may create transport difficulties.

Here is the itinerary I followed:

*Day 1:* Melkhum Trail, Chandranath Hill, Sitakunda Eco Park, the waterfall inside the park and Bashbaria Sea Beach.

*Day 2:* Chattogram city, Bayezid Bostami Shrine, Patenga Sea Beach, Karnaphuli Tunnel, Chattogram Railway Museum and local neighbourhoods.

*Day 3:* Speedboat from Kumira to Sandwip, exploring the island by rented motorbike, and returning to Dhaka by train from Chattogram Railway Station.

This is my personal itinerary, not a guarantee that everyone can complete the same route under all conditions. Build your own schedule around your travel dates, weather, transport availability and the time you want to spend at each destination.

## Final Thoughts: Is Chattogram Worth Visiting on a Budget?

In my experience, Chattogram offers an interesting combination of natural landscapes and urban life. From Melkhum Trail and Chandranath Hill to Bashbaria Beach, Patenga and Sandwip, the destinations added variety to my three-day journey.

If you want to travel on a budget, using trains where practical, staying in simple hotels and checking local transport fares in advance can help manage expenses. However, low cost should not be the only consideration. Safety, time and comfort matter too.

My trip included beautiful destinations and memorable local food, but it also involved some uncomfortable interactions. Those experiences reminded me how important it is to plan carefully and stay aware of my surroundings while travelling alone.

If you are planning to visit Chattogram, my advice is to make a realistic itinerary, check local conditions, keep your belongings secure and prioritise your safety throughout the journey.

*Remember:* A good trip is not just about visiting as many places as possible. It is also about travelling within your means, staying safe and learning from the experiences along the way.
