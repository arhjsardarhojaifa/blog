**Travel date: 9 October 2026**

Some trips are simply about visiting a new place, while others give us a chance to discover something new about ourselves. For me, the Melkhum Trail was one of those experiences. The journey was filled with hills, rocky paths, streams, and cold water, but it also came with fear, uncertainty, and a quiet battle with my own mind.

Most importantly, I completed the entire trail alone. It might sound adventurous, but the experience taught me something important: being able to complete something alone and choosing to do it alone are two very different things.

## The Journey from Chattogram to Melkhum

My one-day trip began in Chattogram, with the Melkhum Trail as my destination. I was excited about the adventure and looking forward to exploring a new place. But no matter how much we learn about a trail beforehand, the actual experience can be very different from what we imagine.

The journey through the Melkhum Trail takes you through hills, rocky paths, and streams. It is not simply a matter of walking forward. You have to pay attention to where you place your feet, what lies ahead, and what is happening around you. Walking across rocks and moving through cold water make the whole experience feel different from an ordinary trip.

There is something unusual about being surrounded by nature like this. On one hand, the beauty of the place draws you in. On the other, the difficult conditions remind you that you cannot afford to be careless.

## A Place That Is Beautiful Yet Frightening

If I had to describe the beauty of Melkhum, simply calling it beautiful would not fully explain how it felt. There is a certain sense of fear mixed with its beauty. The surrounding hills, rocks, and streams create an atmosphere where you can feel amazed by nature while also being aware of your own safety.

That was one of the things I found most fascinating about the place. Experiencing nature up close, making your way through difficult paths, and adjusting to an unfamiliar environment—all of it made the journey feel like a real adventure.

But adventure is not always about enjoyment. Sometimes, it also forces you to understand your own limitations, along with the courage you carry within yourself.

<EditorialImage src={storyPhoto2} alt="A hiker climbing a rocky slope surrounded by lush green foliage" layout="wide" />

## The Moment I Realized I Was Alone

At one point along the trail, I suddenly realized that there was no one near me. That was when a different kind of thought began to cross my mind.

Until then, the beauty of the trail and the excitement of the adventure had kept me moving forward. But when I truly became aware of how alone I was, I started wondering whether I should continue at all. Maybe I should turn back.

It was not an unusual feeling. When you are alone in an unfamiliar environment surrounded by hills and streams, it is natural to start thinking about your safety. At that point, enjoying the scenery is not enough; you also have to pay attention to your situation.

But right at that moment, another thought came from within me: Should I really turn back just because I am afraid?

I did not back down. I carried that uncertainty with me and continued forward.

Looking back now, I feel that moment was one of the most memorable parts of my journey. It was not just about covering a trail; it was also about facing my own fear.

However, overcoming fear does not always mean pushing forward. Knowing when to stop and when to turn back is also a form of courage. I chose to continue that day, but in a different situation, turning back for the sake of safety could have been just as reasonable.

## An Unexpected Encounter with a Snake

One of the most unexpected moments of the entire trip was seeing a snake in the middle of the trail.

I saw it, but I do not know what species it was. So I cannot say whether it was venomous or not. Still, encountering something like that in an unfamiliar natural environment certainly added another layer of excitement to the moment.

While walking through hills, rocks, and streams, you get to enjoy the beauty of nature, but the presence of wildlife is also a natural part of such places. When exploring an environment like this, it is important to stay alert rather than assume you can handle everything on your own.

This is one of the biggest realities of travelling alone. When something unexpected happens and there is no one familiar nearby, you have to rely on your own judgment. And making the right decision is not always easy.

## Cold Water, Rocky Paths, and Moving Forward

Walking through cold water, crossing rocky paths, and moving through the hilly surroundings are some of the experiences I will remember most about the Melkhum Trail.

Trails like this require physical effort as well as an appreciation for the scenery. Not every section is equally easy. Sometimes, the surroundings leave you amazed; at other times, the difficult terrain leaves you exhausted. And when you are alone, you have to manage your own fatigue, fear, and decisions.

In my experience, the joy of this trip was not limited to reaching the destination. The entire journey—the challenges along the way, the moments of fear, the beauty of nature, and those silent conversations with myself—made it memorable.

Eventually, I completed the entire Melkhum Trail on my own. It was a meaningful achievement for me, something I can look back on with pride. But I cannot deny the difficulties, uncertainties, and risks that came with it.

## Is It a Good Idea to Do the Melkhum Trail Alone?

Based on my own experience, even though I completed the trail alone, I would strongly discourage others from attempting the Melkhum Trail solo.

This is not simply a trip to a beautiful destination. The trail can be physically demanding, and dealing with unexpected situations becomes much more difficult when you are alone in an unfamiliar environment. Seeing a snake in the middle of the trail and realizing that no one was nearby made me understand this even more clearly.

Sometimes, we decide to visit a place after hearing adventurous stories or seeing beautiful photographs. But photographs cannot show you how difficult a trail can be, and stories do not always tell you about every possible risk.

So, if you are planning to visit the Melkhum Trail, my advice would be to go with experienced companions, seek help from a local guide, and learn about the current trail conditions beforehand. I do not believe relying solely on your own courage and going alone is a wise decision.

Just because I completed the trail alone does not mean it is safe for everyone else to do the same. Everyone has different levels of experience, physical ability, and circumstances. Your safety should never become less important than the desire to complete a challenge.

## Final Thoughts: A Memory That Will Stay With Me

My one-day journey through the Melkhum Trail will remain more than just an adventure story for me. It was an experience in which I witnessed the beauty of nature, faced my fears, felt the uncertainty within myself, and eventually completed the entire trail alone.

What I will remember most is the moment I realized there was no one around me. Standing between the thought of turning back and the desire to keep moving forward, I had a conversation with myself. That may have been the most personal part of the entire journey.

Even now, when I think about this trip, I remember the hills, the rocks, and the feeling of walking through cold water. I remember that frightening yet beautiful environment where fear and beauty existed side by side.

I completed the Melkhum Trail alone, and that will remain one of the memorable achievements of my travels. But this experience also taught me that showing courage and taking unnecessary risks are not the same thing.

Some journeys introduce us to new places, while others help us understand ourselves a little better. For me, the Melkhum Trail was a mixture of both.

And whenever I think back on this trip, I will remember more than just the happiness of reaching the destination. I will remember the path itself—the path that taught me that to truly appreciate the beauty of nature, we must also respect its power and the risks that come with it.

*For me, the Melkhum Trail is more than just a destination. It is the story of a day spent with myself—a story filled with fear, adventure, and memories that will probably stay with me for a long time.*
